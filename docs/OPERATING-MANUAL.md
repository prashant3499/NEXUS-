# NEXUS — How You Operate the Business

You run everything from **one screen: the Founder Cockpit**, on your laptop.

## Where you operate from
- **The NEXUS desktop app** (built from `desktop/`) → opens straight into the cockpit. This is your command center.
- Or before building the app: `cd backend && node server.js` → open `http://localhost:4100` → **Founder** view.

That cockpit *is* your office. Everything below happens there.

## Your daily loop (15–30 minutes)
1. **Open NEXUS.** You land in the cockpit.
2. **Read "☀ Today's Briefing."** Your 10 AI CXOs + the advisor give you the single top priority and a "do today" list — each with the *why*. This is your agenda, written for you overnight.
3. **Clear the approval queue (HITL).** Anything the engine paused for you — a large payout, a big price change, a fraud flag, a supplier contract, low provenance confidence, a trade filing — is waiting. Approve, reject, or correct each.
4. **Review the engine's proposed fixes.** Auto-correct drafts a fix when it spots an issue; change-control routes any rule change past the protected invariants. You approve; nothing self-applies.
5. **Talk to your AI co-founder.** Ask anything ("what should I focus on?", "how are payouts doing?") or instruct it ("source 20 weavers in Khurja", "draft a cooperative outreach note"). With the Anthropic key set, it reasons over your live data.
6. **Do the human tasks.** The briefing marks some items "[you]" — these are the real-world moves: call a cooperative, follow up with the lawyer, visit a maker, confirm a consent.
7. **Watch the Go-Live panel** tick toward production as you connect each real service.

## Who does what (founder-in-the-loop)
- **The AI runs operations:** sources prospects, drafts listings and outreach, runs the full CXO analysis, monitors health, proposes corrections.
- **You make the calls:** every approval, every relationship (makers, cooperatives, government, lawyer), and the moves only a human can make.
- **Five lines neither of you can cross:** never-in-loss, consent-before-sale, child-safety, no-fabrication, honest-stage. The code enforces them — even against you.

## How a real sale flows once you're live
Auto-source a maker → contact them → **they grant consent** → their craft is listed → a buyer anywhere pays → the platform (as Merchant of Record) handles GST/TCS and compliance → the maker is paid to their bank in ~2 days → you watch it all from the cockpit, approving anything that trips a gate.

## The honest part
You operate the **software** from the cockpit on your laptop.
You operate the **business** with your phone and your feet — the first maker, the cooperative, the government contact, the lawyer's signature.

The cockpit makes you a one-person company with a full management team and an engine that never sleeps. But it cannot make the first phone call to a weaver in Khurja, or sign the legal opinion, or win the pilot. **That part is yours** — and it's the only part that turns this from a running app into a living business.

Start here: open the cockpit, read tomorrow's briefing, and do the one "[you]" task at the top. Then go get one pilot cluster.
