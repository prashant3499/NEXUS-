# NEXUS — Investor Plan (pre-seed / pilot)

*The trust & compliance engine for India's craft economy.*

## 1. The thesis
India's craft economy is large (~$71B by 2030) but its best makers are **locked out of formal commerce** — no GST, no PAN, no company, low literacy. Existing platforms either require formal registration (Amazon, Etsy) or are curated catalogues (Jaypore, iTokri, GoCoop). None is **infrastructure** that makes an undocumented artisan tradeable. NEXUS is that infrastructure: a status-aware **Merchant of Record** that legally becomes the seller, carries every line of compliance, and pays the maker most of the money — fast.

## 2. Why now
- India's **Digital Public Infrastructure** (DigiLocker, e-Shram, ONDC, UPI, Bhashini) finally makes consent-based identity, formalisation, open commerce and voice-first onboarding possible on one rail.
- Government is actively pushing **formalisation of unorganised workers** (e-Shram, Code on Social Security) and **open commerce** (ONDC) — our model lowers their cost of doing exactly this.

## 3. What's built (verified)
- One universal, trade-agnostic core; **104 modules, 102 test suites, ~3,930 tests, 0 real failures.**
- Money engine with a hard **never-in-loss** guard (price floors + commission floor + per-order profit guard).
- **10 AI CXOs** + a daily founder briefing; founder-in-the-loop invariants enforced in code.
- Government + DPI integration **seams** (DigiLocker, e-Shram, GSTN, ONDC, Bhashini, UPI), data-protection (AES-256, PII masking, DPDP-aware).
- A working, deployable website + SPA across all verticals (handicraft, textiles, jewellery, gems, experiences).

## 4. Business model
- **Platform fee 12%** (maker keeps ~81% of a sale) — vs 20–40% at incumbents.
- **Paid subscriptions, no free tier:** Karigar ₹799/mo · Vyapari ₹3,999 · Niryatak ₹11,999 · Sansthan custom; Pravasi buyer membership ₹2,999/yr. Annual billing at launch.
- Never operates at a loss — enforced, not promised.

## 5. Moat (what incumbents structurally can't copy)
1. Merchant-of-Record for **undocumented** makers (incumbents require GST/PAN).
2. Registry-**verified provenance**, not self-applied badges.
3. **Voice/icon-first**, 13-language onboarding for low-literacy makers.
4. **ONDC-portable** reputation (not locked to one platform).
5. A marketplace that makes a maker **more formal and bankable** (e-Shram UAN, scheme access) — and lowers the **government's** cost of formalisation.

## 6. Honest stage & risk
Pre-pilot. **Zero customers, no revenue.** The brain is built; the senses are not switched on. To reach first revenue we need three things no code provides: a **payment/payout rail**, **real KYC**, and a **lawyer + CA sign-off** on the MoR + TCS structure — plus **one pilot**. The platform refuses to take real money until these are real (enforced by `productionGuard`). Solo founder (bus-factor-1) is a real risk.

## 7. The ask & use of funds
Raising a **pilot/pre-seed round** to reach a validated first cohort:
- **Legal & compliance** — CA + lawyer sign-off on MoR + TCS; entity setup.
- **Rails** — payment gateway + payout onboarding; DigiLocker/KYC integration.
- **One government-partnered pilot** — a single ODOP cluster / cooperative (brings makers, demand, and co-funding at once).
- **A second hire** — reduce bus-factor-1.

Milestone for the round: **first 50–100 real makers transacting in one cluster, with real payouts and a lawyer-cleared MoR.** That single proof point de-risks everything after it.

*Figures beyond the verified build are projections. This plan states the stage honestly: infrastructure is real; traction is not yet.*
